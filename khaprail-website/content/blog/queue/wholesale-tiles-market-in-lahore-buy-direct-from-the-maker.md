## What is the wholesale tiles market in Lahore?

**The wholesale tiles market in Lahore is the network of dealers, importers, traders and manufacturers who sell tiles in bulk to contractors, builders and shop owners, usually at a lower per-unit rate than a single-room retail purchase.** Buyers typically deal through a trader or directly with a factory, and the rate depends on tile type, quantity, batch, delivery and the date of the quote.

## Why do buyers choose wholesale instead of retail?

The main reason is the unit cost on large orders, but it is not the only one. A big job needs one batch, because clay and ceramic tiles can vary slightly in shade from firing to firing. A wholesale or manufacturer order lets you reserve the full quantity together, instead of discovering mid-project that the next carton looks different. Wholesale buyers also tend to get a single point of contact for delivery and breakage questions. The trade-off is less hand-holding: trade counters often assume you already know your quantities, sizes and layout. ## What are the layers between a factory and your site?

Most tiles reach a Pakistani building site through one of three routes. The first is a retail showroom, which suits a few rooms and design advice. The second is a trader or dealer who holds stock from several makers and importers, which suits mixed orders and quick pickup. The third is the manufacturer, who sells what it makes directly. Each layer adds handling, storage and margin, and each can add convenience. Neither route is automatically cheaper in every case, so compare real quotes rather than assuming. Be cautious with any page that promises "lowest rate" with no date, because tile rates shift with fuel, transport and demand.

## What decides a bulk tile quote?

A wholesale quote is built from several parts, and two suppliers can differ on any of them.

- **Tile type and size.** A plain square is cheaper to make and sort than a hexagon or an octagon, and clay roof tiles are counted differently from floor tiles.
- **Quantity and batch.** Larger orders may qualify for a different rate, and a single batch protects colour consistency.
- **Finish.** Natural, glazed and handmade finishes take different amounts of work.
- **Delivery.** Distance, loading, unloading and the vehicle used all affect the charge.
- **Timing.** Rates change, so a quote only means something with its date and how long it is held.
- **Breakage allowance.** Ask whether a margin for damaged pieces is included.

## How do you buy tiles wholesale without getting caught out?

Work through a short checklist before you pay anything. First, get your area measured by your mason or architect, with a wastage margin for cuts, since diagonal layouts and many corners use more tiles. Second, ask for a physical sample and look at it in daylight, dry and wet. Third, confirm the size and thickness in writing, because "8x8 inch" can differ slightly between makers. Fourth, ask whether the full quantity comes from one batch and how many spare pieces are included. Fifth, agree on delivery terms, including who unloads and what happens if pieces arrive broken. Finally, keep the quote, which should show the unit, the date and the tile name. ## What should you ask before placing a large order?

Keep a short list ready. Is the quote per piece, per square foot or per thousand? Is the tile natural, glazed or handmade, and does shade vary between pieces? Is the full quantity from one batch? How long is the rate held? What is the lead time if the order is large? What is the replacement process for broken or wrong pieces? How are tiles packed for transport? A supplier who answers these clearly, in writing, is usually easier to work with than one who gives only a headline rate.
