## What are khaprail tiles?

**Khaprail tiles are fired clay roofing tiles, made by shaping natural clay and baking it in a kiln until hard. They are the Punjab's traditional sloped-roof covering and a close relative of terracotta roof tiles.** The tile sheds rain along its curves or ridges and gives a roof a layer of fired clay above the structure.

Because clay is a dense, mineral material, a roof of khaprail tiles tends to feel different from bare concrete under strong sun, though how much cooler a house feels depends on the whole roof build: slope, underlay, ventilation, insulation and ceiling height. No tile works on its own, and any website promising a fixed temperature drop deserves suspicion. We do not publish one.

## How does Faisalabad's climate affect the choice of tile?

Faisalabad generally has very hot summers, a monsoon season with heavy bursts of rain, and cooler winters with occasional fog. In general terms, a roof here has to deal with three things:

- **Strong sun for many months.** Look for an evenly fired tile in a colour you are happy to see faded slightly over the years. Natural red clay is colour-through, so scratches and edge chips do not reveal a different colour underneath.
- **Short, heavy rain.** The roof needs enough slope and overlap to move water off quickly. The tile profile matters less than correct laying.
- **Dust.** Dry, dusty air settles on rooftops. A smoother or glazed surface tends to wash clean more easily with the first rain.

## Natural red or glazed green for a hot, dry city?

There is no single right answer. Natural red tends to blend with brick, stone and plaster, and it ages quietly. A glazed tile has a sealed surface that wipes clean, which helps on a dusty roof, and a glazed colour stands out more from the street.

A practical approach is to take one sample of each and leave them outside in the sun for a few weeks. Rinse them, look at them in morning and evening light, and decide from what you see rather than from a catalogue photo.

## What should you check before you order?

- **Ask for a sample** from the batch you will actually receive, and look at it in daylight.
- **Tap-test a few pieces.** A clear ring usually points to an evenly fired body, a dull sound to a flaw.
- **Check size consistency** by stacking several tiles.
- **Confirm packing and transport**, and what happens to broken pieces.
- **Agree the laying method** with your contractor before work starts, including slope, overlap and fixing.
- **Get the quote in writing**, with unit, inclusions and date.
