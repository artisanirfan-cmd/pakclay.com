## What are khaprail tiles?

**Khaprail tiles are fired clay roofing tiles, made by shaping natural clay and baking it in a kiln until hard. They are the local relative of terracotta roof tiles and have covered homes in the Punjab for generations.** The tile sheds rain, shades the roof below and keeps a traditional look that suits Rawalpindi's mix of older houses, new housing societies and farmhouses.

## Does Rawalpindi's climate suit a clay roof?

Rawalpindi has hot summers, a monsoon with heavy bursts of rain, and cool winters. A clay roof copes with this when the slope, overlap and underlay are right, because water runs quickly off the ridges. In general, a dense, evenly fired tile tends to absorb less water, which helps if damp tiles meet cold nights. ## What sizes and quantities should you plan for?

Size and coverage depend on the profile, so we do not give one number for every tile here. The way to plan a roof is:

1. **Measure the roof surface**, not the floor area. A sloped roof is larger than the room under it.
2. **Ask for the coverage of your chosen profile**, meaning how many pieces cover a given area at the overlap your contractor will use.
3. **Add a margin** for ridges, hips, valleys, cuts and breakage.
4. **Order ridge, hip and edge pieces** along with the field tiles.
5. **Order everything together** so the colour matches across the batch.

Send us your roof dimensions and drawings, and we reply with the profile coverage rather than leaving you to guess.

## What should you check before ordering?

- **See a sample.** Colour and texture vary, and photos hide both.
- **Tap-test several pieces.** A clear ring usually points to an even body.
- **Stack to check size consistency.**
- **Confirm packing and transport** and what happens if pieces break.
- **Agree the laying method** with your contractor before work begins, including slope and fixing.
- **Get a written, dated quote** with the unit stated.

## Natural red or glazed for a Rawalpindi home?

Natural red blends with brick, stone and timber and ages quietly. Glazed tiles are bolder and easier to wipe clean, which can help under trees or in shaded plots where moss settles. Neither wins every time, so leave samples of both outside for a few weeks. You will learn more from that than from a catalogue.
