## Why is buying tiles online riskier than buying a shirt?

Three things make tiles different. First, **shade variation**: natural fired clay differs from piece to piece, and a screen rarely shows the true range. Second, **quantity**: you need an accurate area plus a margin for cuts and breakage, and ordering later from a different batch can give a visible colour difference. Third, **weight and handling**: tiles are heavy and can chip in transit, so packing and delivery terms matter as much as the price.

None of this means you cannot buy online. It means the online step should be a conversation plus a sample, not a one-click payment.

## What should a bulk tile quote include?

A good quote is more than a number. Before you accept any quote, from us or from anyone else, check that it states:

- **The product name, size and finish**, so you know exactly what is priced.
- **The unit**, per piece or per square foot, since mixing the two is the most common reason quotes look cheaper than they are.
- **The quantity covered**, including the margin for cuts.
- **The date it was issued** and how long it stays valid, because rates can change.
- **What is included**: packing, loading, delivery and any taxes.
- **Batch information**, especially if your order will arrive in more than one shipment.

We do not publish a fixed price list on the website, because rates depend on size, shape, finish, quantity, season and distance. We issue dated written quotes for your actual job. ## How do you compare online tile sellers fairly?

Put each offer in the same table: product, size, thickness (ask for it), unit, quantity, date, delivery and return policy. Then compare. A low price per piece on a small tile is not cheaper than a higher price on a larger one if you need twice as many pieces to cover the same area. Ask each seller whether the product is made by them or resold, and whether they can show you a physical sample from the same batch you will receive.

Be careful with listings that show only a rate and a photo, no location, no phone number and no way to see the material. A genuine supplier should be willing to share an address, answer questions on WhatsApp and explain how the tile is fixed and sealed.

## How much should you order?

Measure the area, divide it into rectangles, add the areas, and then add a margin for cuts, patterns and breakage. Patterned layouts and small tiles need a larger margin than plain, large-format ones, and your fixer can advise on the right percentage for your layout. Order everything from one batch so the colour stays consistent, and keep a few spare tiles for future repairs.
