## How do you estimate the cost of a khaprail roof per square foot?

Ask five sellers for the cost of a khaprail roof per square foot and you may get five different answers, and they may not be comparing the same thing. One quote may be for tiles only, another may include battens and labour, and a third may count the plan area rather than the sloped surface. This guide shows how a khaprail roof budget is built, which items to list, and how to compare quotes fairly. We do not print a rate here: Pak Clay has no confirmed, dated per-square-foot figure that would be true for every roof, and a number without a unit and a date can mislead you.

## What are khaprail tiles?

**Khaprail tiles are fired clay roofing tiles, baked in a kiln from natural clay and laid in overlapping rows on a sloped roof.** Because they are a layered system, the cost of a khaprail roof is the cost of the whole build, not just the tile.

## What goes into the cost of a khaprail roof?

A realistic budget has several lines:

- **The tiles themselves.** Priced per piece, per dozen or per square foot, depending on the seller.
- **The roof structure.** A timber or steel frame, or a sloped build-up on a concrete slab, if your house does not already have a suitable slope.
- **Underlay and waterproofing**, where the design calls for it.
- **Battens and fixings.**
- **Ridge, hip, verge and valley pieces**, which are priced separately from the plain tiles.
- **Labour.** Rates differ by city, roof complexity and the contractor.
- **Transport and unloading.** Clay is heavy, and distance matters.
- **A breakage and cutting allowance**, which adds to the tile count.

Add these together and divide by the roof area, and you get your own all-in cost per square foot. That figure is far more useful than a tile-only rate.

## Why does the same tile have different prices per square foot?

Several things move the number:

- **Profile and size.** A tile with a larger exposed area needs fewer pieces per square foot. See our guide on [how many khaprail tiles your roof needs](/blog/how-to-calculate-how-many-khaprail-tiles-your-roof-needs).
- **Finish.** Natural red and glazed tiles are made differently, and glazed options may be priced differently.
- **Quantity.** Larger orders are sometimes quoted at a lower rate, but terms vary, so ask.
- **Distance and delivery.** Lahore buyers and out-of-city buyers face different transport costs.
- **Timing.** Clay product rates can move with fuel, labour and season, which is why a quote needs a date.
- **Roof complexity.** More hips, valleys and cut edges mean more labour and more waste.

## What is the difference between plan area and roof area?

This catches many first-time buyers. Plan area is the footprint of the house, and roof area is the actual surface of the slope. A sloped roof has more surface than its footprint, and the steeper the slope, the bigger the gap. If one quote uses plan area and another uses roof area, the per-square-foot figures cannot be compared. Always ask which area is used and who measured it.

## How do you build your own budget step by step?

1. **Measure the roof surface** along the slope, or ask a contractor to do so.
2. **Choose a profile and get a sample**, so you know the exposed coverage of one tile.
3. **Work out the tile count** and add an allowance for cuts and breakage.
4. **Ask for a dated written quote** for tiles, with the unit stated: per piece, per dozen or per square foot.
5. **Ask your contractor for a separate quote** for frame, underlay, battens, labour and finishing details.
6. **Add transport and a contingency** for the unexpected.
7. **Divide the total by the roof area** to see the all-in cost per square foot.

Keep every quote in writing, with the date, what is included and what is not.

## How do you compare quotes from different sellers?

Put them side by side and check:

- Is the unit the same (piece, dozen, square foot)?
- Is the area the same (plan or surface)?
- Does the quote include ridge pieces, fixings, transport and unloading?
- What is the breakage policy?
- How long is the quote valid?
- Is the tile a real, comparable profile and finish?

A very low rate that leaves out half of these items may end up costing more than a higher quote that includes them.
