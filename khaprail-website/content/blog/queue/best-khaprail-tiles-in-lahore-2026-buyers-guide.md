## What makes a khaprail tile "best" for Lahore?

**The best khaprail tile for Lahore is the one that is evenly fired, sheds monsoon water without pooling, and comes from a maker who will show you the tile in your hand before you order.** Khaprail is the local name for fired clay roof tile, the same family as terracotta roofing. Everything else is detail, so here are the details that matter.

**Firing and clay.** A properly fired tile is hard, gives a clear ring when tapped and has an even colour through the body. A pale, chalky or crumbly tile has usually been under-fired. Ask to see a broken piece if you can.

**Profile.** The shape decides how water leaves your roof. Spanish and French profiles channel rain along curved or interlocking ridges, while flat profiles give a cleaner, more modern line. Each suits a different roof pitch, so choose the profile with your contractor, not after the beams are up.

**Finish.** Natural red is the classic. Glazed tiles, such as a green glaze, resist surface staining and add colour, which some owners prefer on villas and boundary walls.

**Consistency.** On a large roof, tiles that vary in size make a wavy, leaky surface. Check that a handful pulled from different stacks sit flat against each other.

## How does Lahore's climate affect the choice?

Lahore's climate is the reason clay roofing has lasted here for generations. Clay holds heat more slowly than thin metal sheets, so rooms under a tiled pitch tend to stay more comfortable in the afternoon. Tiled roofs also tend to be quieter in heavy rain than bare sheet roofs.

The monsoon is the real test. Water has to leave quickly, so the roof pitch, the overlap between tiles and the quality of the ridge and edge work matter as much as the tile itself. Many "leaking tile" complaints are laying problems, not tile problems. A good supplier will tell you this honestly and point you to a mason who has laid tile before.

## What should you check before you order?

A short checklist saves a lot of regret:

1. **See and touch a sample.** Photos hide colour variation and surface texture.
2. **Tap-test for sound.** A clean ringing note usually means a well-fired tile.
3. **Check size consistency** across several pieces.
4. **Confirm the quantity maths.** Ask how many tiles cover your roof area for the chosen profile and add a margin for breakage.
5. **Ask about delivery.** Tiles are heavy and fragile. Confirm packing, transport to your site and who handles breakages.

## How do you care for a clay roof in Lahore?

Clay roofing asks for little, but not nothing. After the monsoon, have someone walk the roof carefully and look for cracked or slipped tiles, blocked valleys and moss in shaded corners. Replace broken pieces early, because one cracked tile lets water reach the timber or slab beneath. Clear leaves and dust from gutters before the rains, and avoid pressure-washing hard, which can wear the surface. Keeping a few spare tiles from your original batch also helps, since colour can differ slightly between production runs.

## Common mistakes Lahore buyers make

- **Choosing on photos alone.** Colour on a screen is never the colour on your roof.
- **Ordering the exact count.** Always add a margin for breakage in transport and cutting at edges.
- **Ignoring the pitch.** Some profiles need a minimum slope to shed water properly. Ask before you build.
- **Hiring a mason who has never laid tile.** Laying is a skill, and most leaks start there.
- **Mixing batches.** Order the whole roof together so the colour matches.
