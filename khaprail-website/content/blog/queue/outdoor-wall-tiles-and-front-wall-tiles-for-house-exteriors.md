## What are the best outdoor wall tiles for a house front in Pakistan?

The front wall is the first thing visitors see, and in Pakistan it also takes the brunt of the weather: strong summer sun, monsoon rain splash, dust and, in places like Murree, cold and damp. That is why outdoor wall tiles and front wall tiles are chosen differently from interior wall tiles. If you are planning a facade, feature wall or boundary wall, this guide covers the main materials, what each does well, and what to ask before you order.

---

## What are outdoor wall tiles?

**Outdoor wall tiles, also called exterior wall tiles or front elevation tiles, are facing materials fixed to the outside of a building to protect and decorate the wall.** They can be fired clay, natural stone, concrete or ceramic. Unlike floor tiles, they do not carry foot traffic, so grip matters less; weather resistance, fixing method and weight matter more.

## Which materials work for exterior walls?

Each material has its own look and trade-offs. These are general tendencies, so always judge from a physical sample.

| Option | Typical strength | Typical drawback |
|---|---|---|
| Natural sandstone cladding | Rugged natural face, strong texture | Heavier, surface varies from piece to piece |
| Terracotta / gutka clay | Warm traditional colour that does not need painting | Porous, so detailing and drainage matter |
| Terracotta jali | Pattern, shade and airflow for screens | Mostly decorative, not a full wall covering |
| Ceramic / porcelain | Wide range of designs | Usually needs a suitable exterior grade |

## Is natural stone cladding a good idea for a front wall?

Stone cladding is popular because it gives a bold texture without paint. Sandstone tends to look warm and natural, and its uneven face hides dust better than a smooth surface. The honest trade-offs are weight and fixing. Heavier pieces need a suitable adhesive or mechanical fixing and a sound wall behind them. Ask your mason how the pieces will be fixed and whether the wall can carry the load before you finalise.

## Can clay gutka or brick-look tiles be used on a facade?

Yes, small clay pieces are widely used for boundary walls and exterior feature walls, and they give a classic red-brick character. Clay is porous, so a drip detail at the top and a clean line at the base help keep water from sitting against the face. Colour varies slightly from batch to batch, so order the whole quantity together. Our [gutka tile guide](/blog/gutka-tiles-in-lahore-sizes-uses-and-how-to-choose) goes deeper on choosing a piece.

## How do you handle sun, rain and damp on exterior tiles?

Think of the wall as a system. Water should run off, not collect. Good practice generally includes a drip edge or overhang at the top, a sloped sill below windows, correct joint filling and a base that stops rising damp. Shaded north walls tend to stay damp and grow moss, while south and west walls face the strongest sun. A tile alone does not solve a damp wall; the wall behind needs to be sound first.

## How do you choose a look for the front elevation?

Start with the house, not the tile. A plastered white or grey facade can take one strong feature panel, such as a stone-clad entrance bay or a gutka section around the door, without looking busy. Covering the whole front in a single heavy texture can feel flat and dark, especially on a narrow plot where the wall is seen from close up. Natural tones such as sand, terracotta red and warm grey tend to age well because dust and weathering do not show as sharply. Hold two or three samples against the actual wall in morning and evening light before you decide, since colours shift a lot between the two.

## How do you keep exterior wall tiles clean?

Plan on light, regular care rather than occasional heavy cleaning. Dust and bird droppings can be brushed or rinsed off with water. Avoid strong acid cleaners on clay or natural stone unless your supplier says they are safe, because they can damage the surface. Moss in shaded areas is best removed early with a soft brush. If a wall keeps staining in the same spot, look for the water source, such as a blocked gutter or an overflowing tank, instead of scrubbing harder each time.

## What about jali screens on the front elevation?

A terracotta jali is a perforated screen that lets light and air through while giving privacy and shade. It can sit in a boundary wall, a balcony, a staircase void or the front elevation itself. ## How much do outdoor wall tiles cost in Pakistan?

We do not quote a rate here, because a price without a unit and a date can mislead. Exterior wall tile prices usually depend on material, thickness, size, finish, quantity, delivery distance and the season, and may be quoted per piece, per square foot or per thousand. Fixing material and labour are normally separate lines. Ask for a written quote that states the unit, what is included and the date.

## What should you check before ordering?

- **Name the wall.** Front elevation, boundary wall and garden wall differ in exposure.
- **Check weight and fixing** with your mason.
- **Ask for a sample** and look at it in daylight, then wet it.
- **Order the full quantity together** plus a margin for cuts and repairs.
- **Plan drainage details** at the top and bottom of the wall.
- **Get the quote in writing,** with unit, inclusions and date.
