## Which clay and terracotta tiles suit Islamabad weather?

Islamabad has a mix of hot summers, heavy monsoon rain and cool, sometimes frosty winters. In general terms, that makes a few choices important. Roof tiles should be laid with proper slope, overlap and battens. Outdoor floors should drain well and avoid very smooth finishes that can become slippery when wet. Clay is porous, so surfaces in freezing weather deserve extra care; ask about frost conditions for your specific location and piece before ordering.

None of this replaces advice for your own site. Describe your roof or floor, and we will tell you what we would and would not recommend. For roofs in particular, our guide to [khaprail tiles in Lahore](/blog/best-khaprail-tiles-in-lahore-2026-buyers-guide) explains types, fixing and what to ask.

## What should you ask about at the visit?

Bring a short list of questions so the conversation stays practical. Ask for the exact size and thickness of each piece you like, and whether it is meant for roof, floor, wall or all three. Ask how it should be fixed and whether a breathable sealer is advisable. Ask how much extra to order for cuts and breakage, because hexagon, octagon and diagonal layouts need a larger margin than a straight grid. Ask whether the pieces in your quantity will come from one batch, since natural clay can vary in shade between firings. Finally, ask what happens if a few pieces arrive damaged. A supplier who answers these calmly and in writing is usually one you can plan a build around.
