## Cement tiles, concrete tiles or terracotta: which one belongs on your floor?

Ask three shopkeepers in Lahore the difference between cement tiles and concrete tiles and you may get three answers. The words overlap, the products overlap, and terracotta enters the conversation as the "natural" alternative. This guide sorts out the terms, compares the three on the things that matter at home, and shows where each one makes sense. It is written for buyers in Pakistan comparing cement tiles, concrete tiles and terracotta before they spend money.

---

## What is the difference between cement tiles and concrete tiles?

**Cement tiles and concrete tiles are both made from cement-based mixes that are moulded or pressed and cured rather than fired in a kiln.** In everyday talk the two names are often used for the same thing. Some sellers use "cement tile" for thin decorative tiles with coloured surface layers, and "concrete tile" for thicker, plainer pieces such as pavers or otti, but there is no single rule. When you get a quote, ask what the product is made of, how thick it is and what it is meant for.

Terracotta is different in how it is made: clay is shaped, dried and fired in a kiln. That firing step changes the look, the feel and the way the tile behaves with water.

## How do the three compare?

These are general tendencies, not test results. Always check a physical sample.

| Factor | Cement tile | Concrete tile (otti, pavers) | Terracotta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Made by | Pressed and cured | Moulded and cured | Shaped and kiln-fired |
| Look | Often bold patterns and colours | Many shapes, more uniform colour | Warm natural reds and browns |
| Typical use | Interior floors and feature areas | Outdoor floors, paths, driveways | Floors, walls, courtyards, porches |
| Porosity | Porous, usually needs sealing | Porous, texture helps grip | Porous unless glazed, breathes |
| Weight | Often heavy | Often heavy | Varies with size and thickness |
| Look over time | Develops patina, can darken | Can fade or stain | Ages with character |

None of the three is "best". The right one depends on where the tile goes.

## Which is better for outdoor floors?

Outdoors, grip, drainage and thickness matter more than the label. Concrete otti and pavers are widely used for paths, porches and driveways because they are quick to lay and come in many shapes. Textured terracotta is a classic choice for courtyards, verandahs and porches. Thin decorative cement tiles are generally better kept for covered or indoor areas unless the maker confirms an outdoor rating. ## Which is better for heat and rain in Pakistan?

Pakistan's summers are hot and monsoons are heavy, so the more useful question is how a tile behaves when it is hot, wet and dirty. Terracotta tends to feel warm underfoot and does not need paint, but because clay is porous it can darken or collect moss if water stands on it. Concrete and cement tiles can also stain, and many need a sealer. Whichever you choose, a sloped, drained base matters more than the tile name. Test a wet sample outdoors at midday before you buy.

## Which needs more maintenance?

- **Cement tile:** often needs sealing before and after laying to limit stains, and may develop hairline marks over time.
- **Concrete tile:** keep joints tight and sweep out grit; reseal if the surface becomes dusty or stained.
- **Terracotta:** a breathable sealer is optional on many sites; regular sweeping and mopping are usually enough.

Ask your tile fixer what suits your product and site, because the right method depends on the tile and the finish.

## What about roof tiles?

The same question comes up for roofs: clay or concrete. Fired clay roof tiles are our speciality, and clay is generally chosen for its look, its non-rusting nature and its long working life. Concrete roof tiles can be heavier and may cost less at the start. That is a different decision from floor tiles, and it is covered in our [price guide](/blog/khaprail-tiles-price-in-pakistan-what-decides-the-rate) for khaprail roof tiles.

## How much do these tiles cost in Pakistan?

We do not print a rate here, because a price without a unit and date misleads more than it helps. Prices usually depend on:

- the material and how it is made,
- thickness, size and shape,
- surface finish and colour,
- quantity, delivery distance and breakage allowance,
- season, labour and base preparation.

Quotes may be per piece or per square foot, so compare like with like. Measure the area, add a margin for cuts and ask for a written quote that states the unit, what is included and the date.

## Which should you choose?

A simple way to decide:

1. **Indoor feature floor with a bold pattern:** cement tile, sealed carefully.
2. **Outdoor path, driveway or large area:** concrete pavers or otti on a good base.
3. **Porch, courtyard, living area, warm natural look:** terracotta.
4. **Screens and façade pieces:** cement or terracotta jali, depending on the look.

If you are torn, order a sample of each and place them side by side in your own light.
